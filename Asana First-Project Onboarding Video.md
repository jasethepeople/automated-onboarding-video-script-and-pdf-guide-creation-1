# Asana First-Project Onboarding Video

**Format:** Screen-recorded product tutorial  
**Audience:** New individual contributor or small-team lead  
**Run time:** 5:00  
**Primary activation outcome:** The viewer creates a project, adds and schedules a task, and shares the project with a collaborator.

## Creative and UX framing

This asset uses **Asana** as the representative SaaS product. The onboarding journey is intentionally structured around a fast, confidence-building loop: orient the user, create a useful shared container for work, create one actionable task, make its ownership and timing explicit, then invite collaboration. This reflects Asana’s published navigation and task-sharing patterns: the home page surfaces assigned work and recent activity, the sidebar provides access to Inbox, My tasks, and projects, and project sharing is initiated through the project’s **Share** control.[1]

Asana may change labels, menus, layouts, feature availability, and onboarding prompts. ## Recording specifications

Record at **1920 × 1080** or higher, using a dedicated test organization and fictitious identities. Keep browser zoom at 100%, hide unrelated browser tabs, disable notifications, and reset the workspace before the final pass. Screen-level annotations should avoid covering labels or user-entered text; use a high-contrast pointer arrow and a short 6–10 word instruction.

The UI/UX sequence deliberately concentrates on orientation, creation, task clarity, personal prioritization, and appropriate collaboration. Asana documents that new users can reach task creation from **Create**, and that project collaborators are invited through the project’s **Share** button with selectable permissions.[1] The scene choreography is therefore aligned to the intended core journey rather than attempting to survey every feature.
